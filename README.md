# AI Business & Research Prompt Library 🤖📚

A structured collection of **50 AI prompts** designed for practical business problem-solving and evidence-based research.

## 📌 What This Repository Contains

This repository contains two prompt collections:

### 1. 🔍 Citation / AI-Assisted Research

**25 prompts** focused on:

* Research question generation
* Topic breakdown
* Search keywords
* Source comparison
* Claim verification
* Source evaluation
* Statistics verification
* Evidence tables
* Research planning
* Research triangulation
* Citation-based writing
* Final human review

The research collection emphasizes that **AI-generated information is not automatically evidence**. Important claims should be checked against original and reliable sources.

### 2. 💼 Business Prompts

**25 prompts** focused on practical business functions including:

* Marketing
* HR
* Finance
* Operations
* Customer service
* Business decision-making

The business prompts progress from simple tasks to more structured, business-ready prompts involving problems, required data, KPIs, assumptions, verification and human review.

## 🎯 Prompt Progression

Both collections follow a progression:

**Basic → Refined → Research/Business-Ready**

The purpose is to show how a simple AI request can be developed into a more structured prompt with clear context, constraints, output requirements and verification.

## ⚠️ Responsible AI Use

AI should support the research and decision-making process, not replace human judgment.

Users should:

1. Verify important claims.
2. Check original sources.
3. Avoid unsupported statistics.
4. Clearly distinguish facts from interpretation.
5. Review AI-generated recommendations before implementation.

For business decisions, the final decision remains the responsibility of the relevant human decision-maker.

## 👨‍🎓 Purpose

This repository is designed as a practical learning resource for students and anyone interested in using AI effectively for **business analysis, research and evidence-based decision-making**.
